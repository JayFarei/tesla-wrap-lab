TAKE IT TO THE CAR

From studio to Paint Shop.

  1. Select the exact Model Y version and download its PNG. Keep the generated filename.
  2. Tesla app v4.59.0+: Creations → Wrap → Upload. Alternatively, copy the PNG to a folder named Wraps at the root of a compatible USB drive.
  3. In your Tesla: Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps. Select the design and inspect all sides.

PNG, 512–1024 square, no larger than 1 MB. USB: exFAT, FAT32/MS-DOS FAT, ext3 or ext4; no map or firmware updates on the drive. Never format a drive that contains files you need. If menus are absent, check your vehicle/app support. Examples are unofficial fan designs, not in-car certified. Tesla’s instructions ↗
