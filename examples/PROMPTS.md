# Image generation prompts

These four individual artwork tiles were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-01. They are source artwork, not screenshots. The fitting script preserves their detail while composing separate model-specific exports.

## gryffindor

Create a luxury flat printable vehicle DECAL artwork tile inspired by Harry Potter's Gryffindor house. Square composition, rich crimson red and antique gold. Center a noble heraldic lion in an exquisitely detailed engraved metallic shield crest, botanical scrollwork and a few tiny stars. No text, lettering, words or slogans. Flat top-down design, no perspective, no car or UV template. All decoration confined to the central 60% of the image with wide perfectly solid #641c25 background margins, no border on edges. Fine hand-engraved texture and controlled small relief shadows, no global specular shine. This tile will be cut and resized onto a hood and side doors of several Tesla Model Y templates; keep the crest completely inside frame, very readable at small size. Premium magical heraldry, not a cartoon or toy.
